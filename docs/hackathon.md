# Hackathon submission notes

HomHive is submitted as an autonomous household intelligence agent. Its distinctive demonstration is the transformation of verified physical-world evidence into persistent household state and context-sensitive decisions.

## Sponsor technologies

- **Nebius Token Factory:** hosted multimodal vision and NVIDIA Nemotron inference used in the runtime.
- **NVIDIA Nemotron:** advisory planning over eligible household tasks. Deterministic constraints remain authoritative.
- **Tavily:** selective research for unknowns and potential task effort, with links retained when available and associated with the correct evidence.
- **OpenAI and Anthropic:** independent image review for corroborating scene claims.

## Accurate claims

The project contains persistent rooms and objects, AI-reviewed image evidence, autonomous worker orchestration, Tavily integration, and Nemotron advice. Do not describe unverified plant species identification, missing object-specific citations, live robot control, or continuous camera integration as completed features. Historical evaluation results are development-set findings, not general deployment accuracy.
